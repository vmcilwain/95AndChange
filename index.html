Who we are

95&Change LLC is an independent software company based in Maryland. We design and operate web-based SaaS products for owner-operated and early-stage businesses. Companies that need dependable tools but don't have an IT department to run them.

Our work centers on the everyday operational gaps these teams run into: scattered customer records, inconsistent follow-up, manual data entry, and reporting that takes longer to assemble than it does to read.

We keep our product line small on purpose. Fewer products, built carefully, used daily.

How we work

01

Narrow scope

We'd rather solve one workflow completely than ten of them halfway. Our products do a defined job and stay out of the way otherwise.

02

Honest pricing

Straightforward subscription pricing with no long-term contracts. Businesses should be able to understand what they're paying for and leave when it stops being useful.

03

Your data stays yours

Customer data is used to deliver the service and nothing else. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it with advertisers.

What we build

Our current development is focused on customer and lead management tooling: helping a business capture inbound interest, keep records in one place, and follow up on time without juggling spreadsheets.

  • Contact and lead records - one organized place for who you're talking to and what was said.
  • Follow-up workflows - reminders and status tracking so nothing sits unanswered.
  • Customer communication - email and SMS notifications sent only to contacts who have opted in.
  • Simple reporting - a clear view of pipeline and activity, without a setup project.

Products are in active development. If you'd like to be notified when we open access, send us a note.
